Building the Operating System for Fitness Experiences.

Our mission is to unify a fragmented fitness ecosystem and empower both businesses and consumers through AI.

OUR STORY
Fitness businesses deserve software designed specifically for how they operate — memberships, attendance, programs, and growth — not generic tools bolted together.

Consumers deserve one platform that supports every aspect of their fitness journey: workouts, activity tracking, communities, challenges, and AI coaching. STRON connects businesses, communities, and members through an AI-powered ecosystem designed to make fitness easier to manage, more engaging to experience, and more rewarding to sustain.
