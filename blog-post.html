How to scale your virtual challenges to 10k+ users

Sarah Jenkins
• Aug 24, 2026 • 5 min read

Running a virtual fitness challenge is one of the most effective ways to build community, drive engagement, and generate new recurring memberships for your brand. But scaling that challenge from 50 local gym-goers to 10,000+ active participants requires a unified operating platform that removes operational bottlenecks.

1. Automate your onboarding & tracking

When you cross the 500-participant mark, manual data entry, paper sign-ins, and spreadsheet tracking fall apart. Alpha Gym scaled their 30-Day Step Challenge by utilizing STRON's automated onboarding flows. As soon as a user registers, they are automatically placed onto the live leaderboard and sent an automated WhatsApp welcome sequence.

"We used to spend 15 hours a week just updating participant scores and resolving sync issues. Now, STRON handles leaderboard updates and verification in real time, freeing our coaches to focus entirely on member engagement."

2. Leverage viral social proof loops

Sustainable fitness growth relies on viral loops. The most effective way to turn your existing athletes into an organic acquisition engine is by giving them beautifully styled, one-click shareable milestone cards for Instagram Stories and WhatsApp groups.

3. Keep friction at absolute zero

If your fitness event requires athletes to download three separate apps or buy proprietary hardware trackers, drop-off rates surge above 70%. A high-converting event allows members to sync Apple Health, Google Health Connect, or their gym's QR pass in under 5 seconds.
