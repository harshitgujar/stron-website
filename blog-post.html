How to scale your virtual challenges to 10k+ users

Sarah Jenkins
• Aug 24, 2026 • 5 min read

Running a virtual fitness challenge is one of the most effective ways to build community, drive engagement, and generate new leads for your fitness brand. But scaling that challenge from 50 local members to 10,000+ global participants requires a fundamental shift in how you operate.

1. Automate your onboarding

When you cross the 500-participant mark, manual data entry and spreadsheet tracking become impossible. Alpha Gym scaled their 30-Day Step Challenge by utilizing STRON's automated onboarding flows. As soon as a user registers, they are automatically placed onto the leaderboard and sent a welcome sequence.

"We used to spend 15 hours a week just updating participant scores. Now, the platform does it instantly, and we spend that time engaging with our community instead."

2. Leverage social proof loops

Growth relies on virality. The easiest way to turn your existing users into a growth engine is by giving them shareable milestones. When a user hits 10,000 steps, generate a high-quality graphic that they can instantly share to Instagram Stories.

3. Keep the barrier to entry low

If your challenge requires users to download three different apps or buy specific wearables, your conversion rate will plummet. A successful mass-market challenge should allow users to sync their Apple Health or Google Fit data natively with one click.
